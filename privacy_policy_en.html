Animalsdoku – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Summary

Animalsdoku needs no account, does not connect to the internet and collects no personal data from you. The game contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, score, daily streak and challenges, settings (sound, vibration, etc.), the nickname, avatar and frame you pick in your profile and the leaderboard state are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. The other players on the leaderboard are virtual rivals created by the game.

Feedback

The Feedback option opens a draft in your device's mail app. The level you are on and the device type (e.g. android) are added to the draft. The mail only reaches us if you send it; we use it only to answer you and fix bugs.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game shows a notification at 7 pm reminding you of your streak when you have not played yet that day. The notification is scheduled on your device; no server is used. You can turn it off with Settings > Streak reminder or in your phone's notification settings.

Transfer code

The transfer code you create on the account screen is generated on your device so you can move your progress to another device, and it contains your progress. The code only leaves your device if you copy and share it; it is not sent to us.

Deleting your data

You can delete all game data on your device with Settings > Reset progress or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The game is suitable for players of all ages and collects no personal data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published in the app and at the link on the store page.
